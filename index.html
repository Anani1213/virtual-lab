Laboratory Safety Guide

Read before conducting any experiment.

Corrosive agents — Strong acids (HCl, H₂SO₄) and bases (NaOH) destroy tissue. Always add acid to water, never water to acid.
Flammable substances — Hydrogen gas and sodium metal ignite readily. Keep ignition sources at a safe distance.
Explosive reactions — Alkali metals in water release hydrogen violently with intense heat. Never scale up.
Toxic compounds — Copper sulfate and heavy-metal salts are harmful if ingested or inhaled as dust.
Gas evolution — Ensure adequate ventilation whenever gases are produced. Never seal a reacting vessel.
Personal protection — Safety goggles, lab coat and gloves are mandatory in any real laboratory setting.
