Laboratory Safety Protocol

Corrosives — Strong acids and bases cause severe tissue damage. Always add acid to water, never the reverse.
Alkali Metals — Sodium, potassium and lithium react violently with water, liberating hydrogen which can ignite. Never handle without proper shielding.
Explosive Mixtures — Hydrogen–oxygen mixtures detonate on ignition. Ensure adequate ventilation.
Toxic Compounds — Heavy metal salts (lead, mercury, silver) are cumulative poisons.
